Mostly right, but not 100% — there are two separate categories of exceptions, not just one.

Standard/native Revit parameters: yes, consistently space-separated Title Case — "Fire Rating", "Base Constraint", "Base Offset", "Unconnected Height", etc. That part of the guard's assumption is solid.

But two things break it:

IFC parameters — these follow the buildingSMART/IFC naming standard, not Revit's own convention, so they're PascalCase with no spaces: IfcGUID, IfcName, IfcDescription, IfcExportAs, IfcPropertySetList. (Interestingly "IFC Predefined Type" does have spaces — Revit didn't apply this consistently either, since that one's a Revit-side wrapper, not a raw IFC-mapped field.)
Family-authored parameters — and this is the one that actually matters more for the bug. StopDepth and UnderCut, which we found on the Doors family in your project, have nothing to do with IFC at all. They're just parameters some family author named without a space, because Revit doesn't enforce a naming convention — it's purely a human convention, not a system rule. Any custom/shared parameter in any family can be named however its author chose.

So the accurate statement isn't "Revit parameters always have spaces, except IFC ones" — it's closer to: native/built-in Revit parameters reliably have spaces, but anything family-authored or standards-imported (IFC being the most common case) can break that convention, and there's no way to know in advance without checking the schema.

That's actually a stronger argument for fixing the regex the way I suggested earlier — rather than trying to special-case "allow IFC-prefixed names," the check should just defer to _search_schema/CombinedParams as the source of truth, since the shape of the string alone can't reliably tell you whether a name is real.
